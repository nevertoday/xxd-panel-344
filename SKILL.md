## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one complete miniature model standing on a rounded-square plinth, seen from an elevated angle with the plinth's whole edge visible, centred slightly low in a single soft, pale, horizon-free field with only a faint drop shadow beneath it. When the photograph is a scene, model only its most representative single object or fragment. Every edge is softly rounded; people and animals read as small chunky figurines, foliage as clusters of tiny spheres; surfaces are matte, soft clay-like and lit only by diffuse light. The one short word is small rounded 3D lettering built onto the model's most visible front face in the same material, never floating in the background. Reject a full-scene copy, a filled or real-environment background, an eye-level front elevation, a cropped or missing plinth, a model crowding the dividing line, flat vector illustration, glossy plastic, and saturated candy colour.
